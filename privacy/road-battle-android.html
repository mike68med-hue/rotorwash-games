Road Battle (Android) – Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Published by Rotorwash Games

This policy covers the 3D Android game Road Battle (app ID com.rotorwashgames.roadbattle), with the City Streets, Badlands and Rust City campaigns. It explains what information is involved when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have. (Our earlier Android game has its own privacy policy, and the PC game has another.)

Information we collect

We (Rotorwash Games) do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-ins, no ads, no analytics and no servers that receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your driver profiles, garage, cars, in-game money, campaign progress, daily challenge streak and settings on your own phone so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Playing with friends nearby

"Play nearby" lets two phones on the same Wi-Fi network play together. The phones talk to each other directly over your local network; game data (your driver name such as "DRIVER 1", your car, its position and your in-game actions) goes only between those phones and never to a server of ours.

In-app purchase

The game is free to play, with one optional purchase that unlocks the full game. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether you have bought the full game, so it can unlock it, including after a reinstall ("Restore purchase"). Google's handling of your payment is covered by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Road Battle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
