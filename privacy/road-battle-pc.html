Road Battle- City Streets (PC) – Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Published by Rotorwash Games

Road Battle- City Streets is a car-combat action game for Windows PCs, sold on the Epic Games Store, the Microsoft Store and (later) Steam. This policy explains what information is involved when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have. (Our separate Android game has its own privacy policy.)

What data is collected, and how

We (Rotorwash Games) do not run servers, accounts, ads, analytics or in-game purchases, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information. Data is involved in only these ways:

  • Game saves on your computer. Your driver profile, garage, cars, in-game money, campaign progress and settings are written to files on your own PC by the game itself.
  • Your store account. When you buy and launch the game, the store you use (the Epic Games Store, the Microsoft Store or Steam) gives the game an anonymous player ID and your store display name.
  • Epic account sign-in for online play (Epic Online Services). Only if you choose to play online and approve Epic's permission screen, the game receives from Epic your Epic account ID, display name, language, linked account display names, online status (presence), friends list and the list of players you have blocked. It never receives your email, password, real name or payment details.
  • Match data. During an online battle the game sends your display name, chosen car, position and in-game actions to the other players in that match, through Epic's peer-to-peer relay.
  • Crash reports. Only if the game crashes and you choose to send a report: technical details such as your graphics card, Windows version and what the game was doing.

How the data is used

  • Game saves: to let you continue where you left off.
  • Store player ID and display name: to show your name and keep your progress linked to you.
  • Epic account ID, display name, language, linked account display names, presence, friends list and blocked players: only to show your name in online matches, show the game in your language, show which of your friends are playing, let you invite friends or join their games with a join code, and keep players you have blocked out of your games.
  • Match data: only to run the battle you are in.
  • Crash reports: only to find and fix bugs.

We do not use any of this for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell it.

How the data is shared

  • With Epic Games, which provides the sign-in, presence, friends, invites and relay service (Epic Online Services) under its own privacy policy: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy.
  • With the other players in your match: your display name, car and in-game actions, for as long as the match lasts.
  • With the store you bought the game from (Epic, Microsoft or Valve), which handles your account, payment, achievements and cloud saves under its own policy (Microsoft, Valve).
  • We share nothing else with anyone, and we do not sell or rent data.

How long the data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Game saves stay on your computer until you delete the game's save files or uninstall the game (or, if you use store cloud saves, until you delete them through that store).
  • Epic account data (account ID, display name, language, linked account names, presence, friends and blocked players) is used only while the game is running and is not stored by us at all. You can remove the game's access at any time in your Epic account settings under Apps and Accounts. Epic keeps your account data under its own policy.
  • Match data exists only during the match and is not kept afterwards.
  • Crash reports you choose to send are kept only as long as needed to fix the bug, and no longer than 12 months.
  • To ask about or delete any data related to you, email support@rotorwashgames.com.

Your choices

Online play is optional: Campaign, Quick Battle against AI and Free Roam work without signing in to Epic. If you never sign in, no Epic account data reaches the game.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it may offer to send a crash report. A report is sent only if you choose to send it, and contains technical details (such as your graphics card, Windows version and what the game was doing) used only to fix bugs.

Children

Road Battle- City Streets is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
