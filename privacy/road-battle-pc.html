Road Battle- City Streets (PC) – Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Published by Rotorwash Games

Road Battle- City Streets is a car-combat action game for Windows PCs, sold on Steam and the Epic Games Store. This policy explains what information is involved when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have. (Our separate Android game has its own privacy policy.)

Information we collect

We (Rotorwash Games) do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game has no ads, no in-game purchases, no analytics and no accounts of its own.

Information stored on your computer

The game saves your driver profile, garage, cars, money, campaign progress and settings on your own computer so you can pick up where you left off. This information stays on your computer unless your store's cloud-save feature copies it (see below), and it is removed if you delete the game's save files.

Steam and the Epic Games Store

You buy, download and launch the game through Steam (Valve) or the Epic Games Store (Epic Games). These stores handle your account, payment, friends list, overlay, achievements and any cloud saves, under their own privacy policies:

  • Valve: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement
  • Epic Games: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy

The game may receive your store display name and an anonymous player ID from the store so it can show your name in multiplayer and link your progress. We do not receive your email, payment details or real name.

Playing online with friends

When you host or join a multiplayer battle, your computer connects to the other players' computers. As with any online game, the computers in the match can see each other's IP address, and the game sends your driver name, chosen car and in-game actions to the other players. Nothing from the match is stored by us.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it may offer to send a crash report. A report is sent only if you choose to send it, and contains technical details (such as your graphics card, Windows version and what the game was doing) used only to fix bugs.

Children

Road Battle- City Streets is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
