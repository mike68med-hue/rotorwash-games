Simple Chess – Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Published by Rotorwash Games

Simple Chess is a chess game for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it, who collects it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We (Rotorwash Games) do not collect, store or sell your personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-ins and no servers that receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your current game, your game settings and your sound on/off setting on your own phone so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Simple Chess shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Interactions with ads, such as when an ad is shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash data

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and read Google's full privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Remove Ads purchase

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase that removes the banner ad. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether you own the purchase, so the ad can stay hidden, including after a reinstall ("Restore purchase"). Google's handling of your payment is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Consent (Europe, the UK and other regions where required): The app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time using the "Privacy settings" link in the game.
  • Gameplay without ads: The game itself works offline. If your phone has no internet connection, no ads are loaded.

Children

Simple Chess is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
