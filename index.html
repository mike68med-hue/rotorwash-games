About

Rotorwash Games is an independent game studio making straightforward games for Android phones.

Every game is built to respect your time and your privacy: it plays offline, never interrupts you with pop-up or video ads, and we never collect your personal information. Small banner ads from Google AdMob help keep our games free.

Our promise

  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • We never collect your personal info
