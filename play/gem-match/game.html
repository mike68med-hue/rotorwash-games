How to play

Swipe a gem, or tap two gems side by side, to swap them. Line up 3 or more of the same gem to clear them.

Match 4 in a line → an Arrow gem. ↔ arrows clear their whole row, ↕ arrows clear their whole column.

Match 4 in a 2×2 square → a Seeker gem that fires at two more gems of its color.

Match in an L or T → a Bomb gem that blasts the gems around it.

Match 5 in a line → a Rainbow orb. Swap it with any gem to clear every gem of that color.

Swap two special gems together and they react with each other for a huge blast. Two arrows make a T blast!

Get 3 stars on a level to earn a bonus item: 🔨 Hammer (smash any gem), ➕ 5 extra moves, 🌈 Rainbow (turn any gem into a Rainbow orb) or 🦄 Unicorn (gallops across a row, clearing it). Using one never costs a move.

Clear gems in a chain for combo bonuses. Reach the goal before you run out of moves!
